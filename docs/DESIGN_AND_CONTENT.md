# Design And Content Direction

## Design Goal

The site should be polished enough for advertising and investor review, while still feeling practical and grounded in local commerce.

## Visual Direction

- Professional, clean, responsive.
- Strong product screenshots or realistic app mockups.
- Clear section hierarchy.
- Good whitespace without feeling empty.
- Confident color system, not a one-color template.
- Real product and operational signals over generic stock imagery.

Avoid:

- Default Vue starter visuals.
- Technical API diagrams on the public site.
- Heavy decorative gradients or abstract blobs.
- Generic "future of commerce" language without concrete product detail.

## Suggested Homepage Sections

1. Hero with clear Sequo positioning.
2. App preview with customer, merchant, rider, and hub/relay states.
3. How Sequo works.
4. Customer benefits.
5. Merchant benefits.
6. Rider and relay partner benefits.
7. Trust, payments, and delivery accountability.
8. Investor-facing opportunity.
9. FAQ.
10. Contact or waitlist.

## Voice

Use simple, confident, non-technical language.

Good:

- "Shop local, pay digitally, and choose the delivery or pickup option that fits your day."
- "Merchants manage orders from acceptance to handoff."
- "Relay points make pickup and returns easier when direct delivery is not ideal."

Avoid technical backend language. Those details belong in developer or internal docs.
