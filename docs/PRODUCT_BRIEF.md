# Product Brief

## Purpose

`sequoservices.com` introduces Sequo to people who have just discovered it. The site should help them understand the product quickly and feel that Sequo is serious, useful, and trustworthy.

## One-Sentence Positioning

Sequo is a local commerce and logistics platform that connects customers, merchants, riders, and relay points so orders can be paid for, prepared, delivered, picked up, tracked, and returned with accountability.

## Main Audiences

- Customers who want a trusted way to buy from local merchants.
- Merchants who want to sell online and manage orders.
- Riders who want structured delivery missions.
- Relay partners who can receive, store, and release parcels.
- Investors or partners evaluating the business.

## Core Messages

- Sequo brings local merchants online.
- Customers can pay digitally and track order progress.
- Riders and relay partners keep delivery flexible.
- Returns, refunds, commissions, and payouts are managed with clear rules.
- Sequo is built for trust in markets where delivery, proof, and accountability matter.

## Product Capabilities To Explain

- Product discovery and local merchant shopping.
- Digital wallet payment with supported providers.
- Delivery modes: fast delivery, grouped delivery, pickup, and point de relai.
- Merchant order acceptance, preparation, packing, and handoff.
- Rider delivery missions with proof and PIN support.
- Relay point pickup and return flows.
- Bargaining where merchants allow negotiation.
- Cooperative markets where multiple sellers can appear as one market.
- Notifications and real-time status updates.
- Eligible returns within the return window.

## Business Details To Use Carefully

- Sequo is zero-cash by default.
- Backend-supported wallet providers are Yas Togo and Moov Africa.
- Returns have a 72-hour window for eligible goods.
- Merchant commissions are configurable.
- Payout and settlement logic is tracked server-side.

These details can support trust, but the public site should avoid sounding like backend documentation.
